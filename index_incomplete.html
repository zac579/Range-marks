2. GPS Setup

For best results, set the anchor where you hit from, then walk well down the intended target line before setting the target. The farther apart those two points are, the more stable the target direction will be.

GPS measurement settings
Each anchor, target and ball position uses multiple GPS readings and averages them. A fix is flagged as marginal above 8 m accuracy and rejected above 15 m by default. These thresholds are configurable in the code.
